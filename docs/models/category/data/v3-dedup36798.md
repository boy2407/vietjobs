## Manual look
- 20 random `train` titles per class (seed 20260826). No content-free cue found: no URLs, emails or phones in the text, median text length is 721–1,140 characters in every class (no length shortcut), contact words ("liên hệ", "zalo") appear in 2.6–13.6 % of rows without tracking one class.
- The class follows the employer's field more than the job title. "Nhân Viên Kinh Doanh" / "Nhân Viên Tư Vấn" sit in `du_lịch…`, `giáo_dục…`, `marketing…`, `kỹ_thuật_điện…` and `nhóm_nghề_khác` (ids 20065, 10435, 1254, 25421, 24636). `nhóm_nghề_khác` holds IT, accounting and HR titles (ids 24679, 24671, 24732, 24804). Accountants sit in `sản_xuất…` (ids 17403, 19909) beside `tài_chính…`.
- `train.csv` rows are ordered by class (23 category changes along 26,495 rows). Harmless while the training loop shuffles (`batches(..., True, gen)`), but anything that splits or samples by row position would see single-class blocks.
